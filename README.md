# The World Today — Thursday 26 November 2026

A make.fun bounty entry: one image summing up the world's news of
Thursday 26 November 2026.

## What the image shows

A newspaper front page, "THE WORLD TODAY", dated **THURSDAY 26 NOVEMBER
2026** in a red banner. Six stories, each first reported on that day:

1. **Kuwait — Iranian drones kill two Italians in Kuwait.** Two
   technicians for Rheinmetall Italia die at an air-defence surveillance
   station hit by Iranian drones; two more people are wounded.
   Source: Reuters, first reported 26 November 2026.
2. **Strait of Hormuz — Iran says two tankers hit sea mines off Oman.**
   Iran's Revolutionary Guards say two tankers exploded and caught fire
   near Khasab; one supertanker carried over 2 million barrels of crude.
   The claim is unconfirmed. Source: Reuters, first reported
   26 November 2026.
3. **Ukraine — Russia strikes two grain ships near Odesa.** Two foreign
   cargo ships carrying more than 100,000 tonnes of grain are hit
   overnight near Ukraine's Black Sea port, Kyiv says. Source: Reuters,
   first reported 26 November 2026.
4. **Thailand — Blast at Bangkok refinery injures nine.** An explosion
   and fire at Bangchak's Phra Khanong refinery injures nine, three
   critically; all production units are shut down. Source: Associated
   Press / CNN, first reported 26 November 2026.
5. **Indonesia — Mine flood toll rises to 13 dead, 13 missing.** Rescuers
   resume the search in a flooded illegal gold mine in Bogor, West Java;
   13 bodies recovered, 13 miners still trapped 100 metres down.
   Source: Reuters, first reported 26 November 2026.
6. **Britain — UK net migration falls to 77,000.** Official figures show
   the lowest annual net migration since 1997, outside the pandemic,
   down from 204,000 a year earlier. Source: UK Office for National
   Statistics, published 26 November 2026.

## Stories deliberately excluded

These were widely reported on 26 November but were first reported on
25 November, so they are not that day's news and do not appear:

- the United States proposing new tariffs on 14 trade partners;
- the Shenzhou-24 launch carrying the first foreign astronaut to Tiangong;
- the Solomon Islands–Australia security treaty signing;
- the Israeli strike killing Hamas security chief Muhammad Abu al-Awf in Gaza;
- the Cairo ring-road fuel tanker fire;
- the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade postponement (announced 25 November);
- the US federal judge's ICC sanctions ruling (issued 23 November).

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) copies the
shipped `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Both runs write identical bytes.
